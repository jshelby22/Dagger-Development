The family day,
at a glance.

Daywarden keeps the practical parts of household planning visible without turning them into separate apps and scattered lists.

01 / Schedule

Day, week, or agenda.

Create internal events and filter the schedule by household member. Apple Calendar access is optional.

02 / Responsibilities

Clear owners and due dates.

Assign tasks, priorities, and due dates to household profiles, with optional local reminders.

03 / Planning

Shopping meets dinner.

Track quantities and restocks, then keep tonight’s dinner and upcoming meal plans close at hand.

04 / Household

Profiles that feel familiar.

Give each household member a name, color, symbol, and optional photo—without creating online child accounts.

05 / Display

A calm shared view.

Wall Display turns the iPad into an at-a-glance view of the schedule, open tasks, shopping status, and dinner plan.

06 / Commands

Type what the household needs.

Ask about the day or add supported events, tasks, shopping items, and dinners using deterministic rules in the app—not an external AI provider.

Built around
your household.

Daywarden stores its household-planning database on the iPad. Calendar, notifications, photos, speech, and music access are optional and tied to the features you choose.
