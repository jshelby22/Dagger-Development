Unpin Privacy Policy

Effective October 1, 2026

About this policy

Unpin is an emotional check-in and affirmation app by James Shelby, doing business as Dagger Development. This policy covers the Unpin iOS app, its subscription services, and support communications.

Your reflections stay on your device. Subscription and support services do process information, as described below.

Reflections stored on your device

Your emotional check-ins, saved moments, affirmations, and patterns are stored locally on your device. Unpin does not send this content to Dagger Development or RevenueCat. Unpin does not require an Unpin account and does not provide cloud sync for reflections.

The local reflection data is excluded from device backups. Do not rely on reinstalling the app, restoring a backup, or changing devices to recover it.

Subscriptions: Apple and RevenueCat

Apple handles payments through the App Store. Dagger Development does not receive your payment-card details.

Unpin uses the official RevenueCat iOS SDK to validate purchases, manage subscription access, restore purchases, and provide purchase analytics. RevenueCat processes an automatically generated anonymous app user ID, purchase history, and subscription status. An anonymous app user ID is a technical identifier, not a promise that all purchase-related data is anonymous or that no data is collected.

Unpin does not supply RevenueCat with a custom account ID, your name, email address, or other contact attributes. Your feelings, check-ins, affirmations, and saved reflection content are not sent to RevenueCat. The SDK and its services may also process technical information needed to operate, such as app and device information and network request information.

Learn more in RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy and Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Advertising and analytics

Unpin does not use advertising SDKs or cross-app advertising tracking. Other than RevenueCat’s subscription and purchase analytics described above, Unpin does not include an additional analytics SDK. We do not sell your reflections or use them for advertising.

If you contact support

If you email daggerdevelop@gmail.com, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to respond to your request and may retain correspondence as reasonably needed for support and business records. Please do not send sensitive reflections, passwords, or payment-card information.

Deleting data and managing your subscription

Use Start Fresh in Unpin to delete your saved moments and patterns on that device. Start Fresh keeps your app preferences. Deleting the app removes its local app data; offloading an app is not the same as deleting it.

Start Fresh and deleting the app do not cancel a subscription or delete Apple’s purchase records or RevenueCat’s subscription records. Manage or cancel your subscription through Apple’s subscription settings. Apple and RevenueCat may retain transaction information according to their own policies and applicable obligations.

For a privacy request concerning information held by Dagger Development or our subscription service, email daggerdevelop@gmail.com. We can review the request and explain available next steps; we cannot promise deletion of records controlled by Apple or other providers.

Changes and contact

We will update this page and its effective date when Unpin’s data practices change. For privacy questions, contact James Shelby / Dagger Development at daggerdevelop@gmail.com.
