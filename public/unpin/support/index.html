Unpin Support

Updated October 1, 2026

Contact support

Email daggerdevelop@gmail.com for help with Unpin. Include your app version, device model, iOS version, and a brief description of what happened. If you send a screenshot, remove private content first.

Please do not send sensitive reflections, passwords, Apple Account credentials, or payment-card information.

Subscription and free trial

Unpin’s monthly subscription is offered in the United States at US $3.99 per month, with a seven-day free trial for eligible users. Full access after the trial requires a subscription. Check Apple’s purchase confirmation sheet for your eligibility and the terms that apply before confirming.

The subscription automatically renews unless canceled. If you start an eligible free trial, it converts to a paid monthly subscription unless canceled before it ends. To avoid a charge, cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or current subscription period ends.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Unpin, then choose the available cancellation or management option. You can also open Apple’s subscription settings. Deleting Unpin or using Start Fresh does not cancel your subscription.

How do I restore access?

Use Restore Purchases in Unpin while signed in to the Apple Account used for the purchase. An internet connection is needed to check your subscription. If access is still missing, check that the subscription is active in Apple’s settings and email support with a description of the issue. Restoring purchases restores eligible subscription access, not deleted reflections.

How do I request a refund?

Apple handles billing and refund decisions. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com to review available options. Dagger Development cannot guarantee or issue an App Store refund directly.

Accounts, storage, and deletion

Do I need an Unpin account?

No. Unpin does not require an app account. Apple manages your purchase, and RevenueCat helps validate subscription access using an automatically generated anonymous app user ID.

Are my reflections synced or backed up?

No. Check-ins, saved moments, affirmations, and patterns stay on the device. There is no cloud sync of reflections, and the local reflection data is excluded from device backups. Changing devices or reinstalling the app will not restore that history.

What does Start Fresh delete?

Start Fresh deletes your saved moments and patterns while keeping your app preferences. This cannot be used to recover earlier reflections. Deleting the app removes its local app data; offloading it is not the same as deleting it. Neither action cancels a subscription or deletes Apple or RevenueCat transaction records.
