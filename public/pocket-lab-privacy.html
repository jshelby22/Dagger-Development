Alchemy Game: Pocket Lab

Overview

This policy applies to Alchemy Game: Pocket Lab (“Pocket Lab”), developed by Dagger Development.

Gameplay data

Pocket Lab stores discovered ingredients, completed requests, onboarding state, and related progress locally on your device. Dagger Development does not receive this gameplay progress. Deleting the app removes local gameplay data, subject to backups managed by Apple.

Purchases

Pocket Lab offers an optional one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase. Payments are processed by Apple. Pocket Lab uses RevenueCat to validate purchases, unlock paid content, and restore purchases. RevenueCat receives purchase history and an anonymous app user identifier for these purposes and for aggregate purchase analytics. Pocket Lab does not create user accounts or send RevenueCat your name, email address, advertising identifier, or gameplay progress.

Apple and RevenueCat process information under their own privacy policies and terms.

Advertising and tracking

Pocket Lab contains no advertising SDK, does not request permission to track, and does not use purchase information to track you across apps or websites.

Children’s privacy

Pocket Lab does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app has no account system, social features, chat, or user-generated content.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to daggerdevelop@gmail.com.
