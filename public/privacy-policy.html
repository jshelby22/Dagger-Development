Read this with the app you use. This is Dagger Development’s universal privacy and support policy. Individual apps can have additional, more specific disclosures in the App Store listing, in the app, or on a linked product page. If those details differ, the app-specific disclosure controls for that app and version.

Who this applies to

This policy applies to iOS apps and games published by Dagger Development. It explains the common ways information may be handled when you use an app or contact Dagger Development. Data practices vary by product and feature, so use the App Store privacy label, permission prompt, and any app-specific notice for the version you download.

Information in the app

Many Dagger Development apps keep the content you create—such as app settings, gameplay progress, records, notes, photos, or saved items—on your device. A specific app may also offer a feature that uses an Apple system service or an external service you choose to connect. When that happens, the app’s own disclosure and the third party’s policy apply.

On-device data

Local app content is generally available only on the device where you save it, subject to backups you manage with Apple or another provider.

Optional features

Some features require a service, a permission, or information you actively choose to provide. They are described when you use them.

Permissions

Depending on the app and the feature you choose, iOS may ask for permission to use device capabilities such as photos, camera, location, notifications, microphone, speech recognition, calendar, or media controls. Dagger Development apps request a permission only when a feature needs it. You can decline optional permissions or change them later in iOS Settings, though a related feature may not work without access.

Purchases

Apple processes App Store payments. Apps that offer an in-app purchase may use RevenueCat to load product information, validate a purchase, restore access, and maintain entitlement status. For those purchase functions, RevenueCat may process an app-specific identifier, product and transaction information, entitlement status, and limited app, device, or request metadata.

Apple and RevenueCat handle their own information under their respective policies. See Apple’s privacy information and RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

Sharing and third-party services

An app may create a file, image, message, or other item only when you choose to export or share it. You choose the destination in the system share sheet. If you intentionally connect an app to a third-party service, that service may receive the information needed to provide the feature under its own terms and privacy policy.

Support communications

When you email Dagger Development, we receive the email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to respond to your request and may retain it as reasonably needed for support and business records. Please do not send passwords, Apple Account credentials, payment information, or sensitive content unless it is necessary and you have been asked for a redacted example.

Get support for any app

Email daggerdevelop@gmail.com with the app name, app version, iPhone or iPad model, iOS or iPadOS version, what you expected, and what happened. This lets Dagger Development route your question to the right app without asking for private account credentials or payment details.

Your choices and deletion

You can manage optional permissions in iOS Settings and delete content within an app when that app provides a deletion control. Removing an app generally removes its local app data from that device, subject to backups you manage. Files you deliberately export, share, or save with another service remain where you sent them. Apple and purchase providers retain purchase records as needed to validate and restore purchases under their policies.

Children’s privacy

Use the age rating and product information in the App Store to decide whether an app is appropriate. Dagger Development does not knowingly collect personal information from children through its own support or product systems without the required notice and consent.

Changes

If a Dagger Development app’s data practices change, the applicable public policy, in-app notice, or App Store privacy information will be updated before or when the change takes effect. The date above reflects the latest update to this universal page.

Contact

For privacy questions or support with any Dagger Development app, contact daggerdevelop@gmail.com.
