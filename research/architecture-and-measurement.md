# DaggerDev Astro Migration and SEO Architecture

## What changed

The repository previously published plain HTML directly from the repository root. This phase introduces a minimal Astro static-site build without redesigning the DaggerDev identity:

- Existing colors, typography, grid texture, brand mark, homepage story, principles, contact details, and legal/support content are preserved.
- Existing public legal/support and Daywarden routes remain copied verbatim from `public/`.
- New homepage, app directory, and app detail pages are generated by Astro.
- App detail content is centralized in `src/data/apps.ts` and rendered through reusable components/layouts.

## Adding a future app

1. Add the current icon and App Store screenshots under `public/assets/`.
2. Add or update a source-grounded app record in `src/data/apps.ts` and the public-portfolio record in `src/data/portfolio.ts`.
3. The dynamic `/apps/[slug]/` route automatically uses the shared page system.
4. Rebuild and validate metadata, content claims, structured data, links, and images.

## Search and discovery

- `@astrojs/sitemap` generates the sitemap from `site: https://daggerdev.com`.
- `public/robots.txt` permits crawling and advertises the sitemap index.
- Each Astro page receives a unique title, description, canonical, Open Graph metadata, Twitter card metadata, and one visible H1.
- Individual app pages emit source-grounded `SoftwareApplication` JSON-LD.
- FAQ content is visible and semantic, but FAQ structured data is intentionally omitted; it is not necessary for this product-site use case and avoids implying rich-result eligibility.

## Analytics and App Store attribution

No analytics dependency was added. This avoids silently introducing tracking or a cookie/consent burden.

Recommended next measurement setup:

1. Create App Store Connect campaign links for DaggerDev website CTAs after choosing a stable campaign naming convention.
2. Keep canonical App Store product URLs as the source and generate campaign variants through Apple/App Store Connect rather than inventing query parameters.
3. If site-level click and referrer measurement is desired, approve a small privacy-conscious platform separately (for example, a cookieless aggregate analytics product) after reviewing its data-processing terms.
4. Once approved, attach the same CTA event name and location (`hero`, `footer`, directory) to the existing `data-app-store-cta` attributes.

Do not add campaign parameters until the exact links have been created and verified in the account; the current production links remain clean, direct Apple URLs.
